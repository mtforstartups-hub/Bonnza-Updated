# Bonnza Ecommerce Business Idea Taxonomy

Initial editorial catalogue prepared 18 November 2026.

This structure contains 15 departments, 100 selling categories, 500 example product subcategories and 100 starter business ideas. It organises the supplied retail outline for business discovery. It does not claim to be an official or exhaustive catalogue from any marketplace.

Capital and operating classifications are proposed editorial assessments. They help compare possible small tests. They are not verified investment requirements, demand research, profit estimates or approval to list a product. Individual variants can have different requirements.
